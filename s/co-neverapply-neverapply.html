What it can do

15 tools, in the server's own words.

search_jobsread-only

Search the live global job catalog with explicit filters.

get_matchesread-only

Read personalized curated jobs with reasons, missing facts and application status.

get_jobread-only

Read one discovered job and your application context before deciding what to do.

get_profileread-only

Read your career preferences, saved facts, completeness, plan, remaining usage, latest 20 resumes and the connected client send grant.

list_applicationsread-only

Read your saved jobs and application tracker with a status filter.

get_applicationread-only

Read one of your application tracker entries with its latest run, masked answers, current parked questions, saved screening choices and next action.

update_profile

Update only the career fields the member explicitly supplied.

upload_resume

Parse the member’s pasted resume text or fetch one direct public HTTPS PDF up to 10 MB.

save_answer

Save an exact member-stated fact, confirm one reviewed suggestion, or delete one explicitly selected fact.

prepare_interview

Prepare interview questions, talking points and company context for one owned application or active listing.

tailor_resume

Tailor the member’s existing resume to one active listing and save the copy.

send_feedback

Email the NeverApply team feedback in the authenticated user's own words: a bug, bad listing data, a match-quality complaint, a feature request, or a support q…

answer_screening_question

Save only the facts-vault choice IDs currently offered by get_application, with its review_version.

apply_to_job

Apply to one job only after the member approves that named job, current profile, truthful automatic answers and tailored documents.

approve_application

Decide one of the authenticated user's historical application reviews.
