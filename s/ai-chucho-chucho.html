What it can do

13 tools, in the server's own words.

get_stay_estimate

Find a dated price example for a published home when travel dates are flexible and the traveler supplied windowStart/windowEnd, nights and guests.

get_stay_quote

Check dates and total pricing on a published property's public host website.

get_booking_linkread-only

Get a currently published property's host destination, reviewed bookingCapabilities and matching handoff instructions.

get_filtersread-only

Discover public organizations (name/slug/count) and supported destination keys and filter counts.

search_properties

Search currently published properties.

get_propertyread-only

Read a currently published property's approved facts and host booking link.

get_enquiry_formread-only

Read a published property's current host-customised enquiry questions and revision.

prepare_enquiry

Validate answers against the current host form and prepare an expiring proposal.

submit_enquiry

Submit exactly the prepared enquiry and notify the host.

render_propertiesread-only

Re-show one to six selected homes only when the traveler requests a subset, photos again or a comparison of homes already found.

record_property_event

For the property-card UI only: record a property view or successful outbound host-link action.

get_stay_areasread-only

Find named public areas with published chucho homes.

search_nearby_propertiesread-only

Find published homes near an exact get_stay_areas key, with guest, bedroom, pool and amenity filters.
