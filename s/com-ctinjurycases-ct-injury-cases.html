What it can do

14 tools, in the server's own words.

describe_datasetread-only

Start here.

search_casesread-only

List auto-injury cases matching filters, in docket-number order, up to 50 per page.

get_caseread-only

One case: dates, disposition, trial, verdict and offer amounts, its motion rulings, and the judges, attorneys and law firms by side.

statsread-only

Compute one metric over cases (or motion rulings), optionally broken down by up to two dimensions and narrowed by filters.

search_judgesread-only

List judges by name and district, with case counts, most cases first.

get_judgeread-only

One judge: how the cases they decided ended (closures in the last ~11 months), motion grant rates by motion family, and trial and verdict figures.

search_firmsread-only

List law firms by name, side, district and size, most cases first.

get_firmread-only

One law firm: its attorneys, the firms it most often faces, the medical experts named for its side, its motion practice, and how its cases ended.

search_attorneysread-only

List attorneys by name, firm, side and district, most cases first.

get_attorneyread-only

One attorney: their firms, the firms they most often face, the medical experts named for their side, their motion practice, and how their cases ended.

search_providersread-only

List medical providers named in expert disclosures, by type, specialty, side (treating or defense examiner), town and size, most cases first.

get_providerread-only
