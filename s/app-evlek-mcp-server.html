What it can do

8 tools, in the server's own words.

search_listingsread-only

Use this when the user wants to find or see property listings; results are shown with real photos in the Evlek widget.

searchread-only

For research and citation lookups (deep research): free-text search of live Northern Cyprus (KKTC/TRNC) property listings on Evlek, in Turkish, English, Russia…

fetchread-only

Fetch the full detail of one Evlek listing: title, price, location, rooms, areas, amenities, data-quality notes and the canonical link.

get_listingread-only

Get the full details of ONE public Evlek listing (sale or long-term rent) in the user's language.

compare_listingsread-only

Compare 2 to 4 public Evlek listings side by side in the user's language: price, price per m² (only when the built area is believable), rooms, bathrooms, built…

market_statsread-only

Market statistics for Northern Cyprus from asking prices of active public listings (not sales, valuations or forecasts).

list_locationsread-only

List the places Evlek knows in Northern Cyprus (6 cities, districts, villages, sub-areas, landmarks, the Karpaz region) with their public sale and long-term re…

convert_currencyread-only

Converts an amount between GBP, EUR, USD and TRY with the exchange rates Evlek stores (refreshed daily) and returns all four equivalents.
