What it can do

5 tools, in the server's own words.

search_productsread-only

Search every garment Levels sells by category, brand, trade, colour, fabric, budget and more.

get_productread-only

The facts of one garment: brand, fabric, fit, sizes, colours, what it suits, its price before a logo and its link.

get_quoteread-only

A real price from the site's own pricing engine for one garment at a quantity, with one logo on the left chest at a typical size.

get_business_factsread-only

Timings, delivery, minimums, set-up, decoration methods, samples, returns and contact details, as the site states them.

start_orderread-only

The link that takes the customer to a garment to add their logo, choose sizes and pay, and the link to ask for a sample first.
