What it can do

4 tools, in the server's own words.

certscore_scan_site

Use for a user-requested website launch review, vendor assessment, cookie/tracker inventory, consent/GPC investigation or privacy-policy review.

certscore_get_scan_statusread-only

Returns lifecycle status for a stable CertScore scanId.

certscore_get_report_evidence_pageread-only

For a focused question, use section=cmp, cms, consent, gpc, policy, tracking, transport, forms or accessibility.
