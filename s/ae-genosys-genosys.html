What it can do

11 tools, in the server's own words.

search_productsread-only

Search the live GENOSYS UAE catalog (Korean professional skincare, haircare, devices, beauty boxes).

get_productread-only

Full details for one product: description, key features, benefits, key ingredients and actives, step-by-step how to use, sizes or shades with prices, and an ad…

recommend_routineread-only

Ranked product picks for a shopper's skin type and concerns, the same scoring as the skin quiz on genosys.ae.

list_categoriesread-only

All product categories and skin concerns in the live catalog, with product counts.

create_bag_linkread-only

Build one link that opens genosys.ae with these items already in the shopper's bag, ready to check out.

open_checkoutread-only

Show the shopper a checkout form for these items: shade picker where needed, quantities, delivery details (name, email, phone, emirate, address), live total an…

place_order

Create a GENOSYS UAE order from this chat and get a secure Stripe payment link (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay).

order_statusread-only

Status and payment of a GENOSYS order.

store_inforead-only

Delivery areas, times and fees, payment methods, returns, contact (WhatsApp, email), apps and policies for GENOSYS UAE.

searchread-only

Search GENOSYS products and skincare articles.

fetchread-only

Full text of a result from search, by id ("product:18" or "blog: ").
