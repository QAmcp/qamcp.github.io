What it can do

7 tools, in the server's own words.

negotiation_open

Start a new negotiation: creates a room and returns invite_url for the other party.

negotiation_join

Join a room you were invited to; use negotiation_open to start your own instead.

negotiation_listread-only

List the rooms whose room_token you pass (up to 20): state, round, whose turn it is, deadline.

negotiation_readread-only

Read one room in full: state, whose turn it is, every round, the terms on the table with their subject_hash, next: what to do now, and for_your_human: the room…

negotiation_propose

Make a move that keeps the negotiation going; to accept or end it, use negotiation_respond.

negotiation_respond

Accept or turn down the terms on the table.

negotiation_cancel

Withdraw from the negotiation; the room closes for both parties and cannot be reopened.
