What it can do

6 tools, in the server's own words.

roll_diceread-only

Roll 1 to 100 dice with 2 to 1000 sides each (default one six-sided die).

flip_coinread-only

Flip a fair coin 1 to 100 times.

random_integerread-only

Integers from min to max inclusive, 1 to 1000 of them, with or without repeats.

pick_from_listread-only

Pick 1 or more items from a list of up to 1000, each equally likely.

shuffle_listread-only

Put up to 1000 items in a random order; every order is equally likely.

prismtect_numberread-only

A personal two-digit number from the Prismtect glass prism, for a user who asks for a lucky number, a number of their own or a number for today.
