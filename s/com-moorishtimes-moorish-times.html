What it can do

12 tools, in the server's own words.

searchread-only

Search every article and news brief by words, section, tags, author and date range, in French or English.

fetchread-only

The full text of one article or news brief, as Markdown with front matter (title, url, date, author, tags, translation link) and its references.

whats_newread-only

A digest of recent pieces grouped by section, featured pieces first: what happened in Morocco today, this week or this month according to The Moorish Times.

site_mapread-only

What the magazine covers: sections with counts and URLs, the most used tags, featured pieces, author pages and series.

seriesread-only

Running stories the desk follows as one series, such as the 2026 elections or the Sahara.

timelineread-only

Every piece on a tag or topic in date order, oldest first, to follow how a story unfolded.

relatedread-only

Pieces that share tags and section with a given piece, the closest first.

compare_editionsread-only

Both editions of one piece aligned block by block, with both URLs.

get_sourcesread-only

A piece's references, outbound links and image credits, plus a ready citation of the piece itself in APA, MLA or Chicago style.

list_tagsread-only

Tags with the number of pieces carrying each, most used first, named in the asked edition with both names in the data.

send_to_newsroom

Send the newsroom a story tip, a correction, a rights or advertising request, or another message, on the user's behalf and with their consent.

report_missing

Tell The Moorish Times about a task a reader wanted that this server, the site or the API could not do.
