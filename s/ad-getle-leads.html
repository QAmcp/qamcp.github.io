What it can do

35 tools, in the server's own words.

get_accountread-only

Plan, entitlements and remaining credits for the connected Getlead workspace.

list_mailboxesread-only

Connected sending mailboxes (SMTP, Gmail OAuth, API providers) with their id, from address and active flag.

test_mailbox

Send Getlead's connection test for one mailbox to confirm it can authenticate and send.

list_campaignsread-only

Every campaign in the workspace with status and send counts.

get_campaign_statsread-only

Delivery and engagement counters for one campaign: sent, failed, opens, clicks, replies.

get_campaign_analyticsread-only

Open, click, reply and bounce breakdown over time for one campaign.

control_campaign

Change the run state of a campaign.

create_campaign

Queue a cold email campaign against a lead list.

crm_leadsread-only

Leads in the CRM pipeline with their stage and value.

crm_update_lead

Change a CRM lead's pipeline stage, deal value or notes.

check_deliverabilityread-only

Inspect SPF, DKIM, DMARC and MX for a domain.

suppression

Read suppression stats or add addresses that must never be emailed again.

email_templatesread-only

Read the workspace's saved email templates so a campaign can reuse proven copy.

get_inboxread-only

Replies received across every connected mailbox, newest first.

get_threadread-only

The full conversation with one lead: what was sent, what they replied, and the tracking events.

draft_replyread-only

Ask Getlead to draft an answer to a lead using the conversation context.

send_reply

Send a real email to a lead from the mailbox that owns the conversation, threaded onto it.

list_opportunitiesread-only

Replies Getlead classified as opportunities, with their status and estimated value.

update_opportunity

Change the status, estimated value or notes of an opportunity.

preview_b2b_leadsread-only

search_b2b_leads

enrich_lead

Look up a person or company and enrich it with public profile and company data (role, company details, social profiles).

scrape_leads_start

Queue a background scrape for fresh leads from the web and social platforms.

scrape_job_statusread-only

Progress of a scrape job started with scrape_leads_start.
