A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

18 tools, in the server's own words.

search_transit_inforead-only

Full-text search of the agencies' web pages: fares, how to pay, accessibility, safety, rules, new-rider/visitor guides, and service-change notices.

get_service_alertsread-only

Live service disruptions: WMATA incidents (Metrorail, Metrobus, elevators) and agencies' GTFS-realtime alerts (detours, closed stops, missed trips).

get_rider_reportsread-only

Recent reports from other RIDERS (crowdsourced on this site): delays, crowding, broken elevators, tips.

get_elevator_escalator_statusread-only

Elevators and escalators currently out of service, optionally for one station.

get_station_inforead-only

Lines served, address, parking availability, transfers, and entrances for a station (accepts a name like "Gallery Place" or a code like "B01").

get_fareread-only

Station-to-station Metrorail fare.

geocode_addressread-only

Find where a street address, hotel, attraction or other place is, in the DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia area.

plan_tripread-only

get_metro_routeread-only

How to ride Metrorail between two stations: which line(s), where to change trains, which direction ("toward ..."), number of stops, and the fare.

list_topicsread-only

List content categories with document counts and data freshness.

get_guideread-only

Full text of the primary guide for a topic: new_rider, visitor, payment, fares, accessibility, safety, or maps.

list_agenciesread-only

find_stops_nearread-only

Stops and stations within walking distance of a place, across all agencies.

get_routeread-only

One route from an agency's GTFS schedule: days and hours of service, typical frequency, endpoints, fare, and any live disruptions on it.

transfers_atread-only

Routes from OTHER systems reachable on foot from a station or stop, grouped by agency, each with walk time and its service window from the GTFS schedules.

how_to_payread-only

How to pay to ride one agency.

plan_connectionread-only

estimate_trip_costread-only

Estimate the total fare for a cross-system trip: per-leg fares, how to pay each, and a total — noting each leg is usually a separate payment.
