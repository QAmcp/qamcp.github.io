What it can do

19 tools, in the server's own words.

search_siteread-only

Searches the whole publication at once and says what kind each hit is: news articles, First Light items, entity records, projects, source documents and tracker…

search_articlesread-only

Full-text search of published GMM news articles (title, summary, body), newest first, with an optional desk (section slug) and date range.

list_recent_articlesread-only

The newest published GMM news articles, optionally from one desk (section slug).

get_articleread-only

The full text of one published article as markdown, with headline, byline, key takeaways and references, ending in its canonical URL.

get_page_markdownread-only

Reads any public GMM page as clean markdown, the same text the site serves at the page address with .md appended.

get_first_lightread-only

One edition of First Light, the daily morning brief, as markdown: its key number and numbered snippets with links.

list_first_lightread-only

The archive of published First Light morning briefs, newest first, with each edition date, title and snippet count.

list_entity_categoriesread-only

The categories GMM files its entity records under — people, companies, institutions, places and the rest — with each one's slug and how many records it holds.

search_entitiesread-only

Searches GMM's researched entity records (people, companies, institutions, places) by name, role or summary.

list_entitiesread-only

The index of GMM entity records, grouped by category (people, companies, institutions, places), optionally limited to one category slug — list_entity_categorie…

get_entity_recordread-only

GMM's researched record of one entity: the dossier as markdown, plus the full record as structured data.

search_documentsread-only

Searches primary-source documents (laws, budgets, contracts, reports) by title, summary or reference.

list_documentsread-only

list_projectsread-only

GMM project tracker: public projects with status, value, employer and contractor, newest first.

get_projectread-only

One tracked project: parties, value, financing, status against schedule, variations, documents and coverage, as markdown ending in its canonical URL.

list_tracker_seriesread-only

The economy tracker's catalogue: Maldives Monetary Authority statistical series grouped by theme, each with its latest figure and year-on-year change.

get_tracker_seriesread-only

One economy tracker series: unit, frequency, source, the revision versions MMA has published and the latest observations.
