What it can do

12 tools, in the server's own words.

top_stories

Fetch the current Hacker News front page (top stories) via the official Firebase API.

new_stories

Fetch newest Hacker News story submissions.

best_stories

Fetch best-ranked Hacker News stories.

get_item

Get one Hacker News item (story or comment thread) by numeric id, including top-level comments when available.

get_user

Look up a Hacker News user profile (karma, about, created date) by username.

browse_feed

Browse an HTML feed on news.ycombinator.com: news, newest, ask, show, jobs, newcomments, front, or best.

search_stories

Search Hacker News stories via the public Algolia API (same backend as hn.algolia.com).

login

Sign in to Hacker News with username and password.

session_status

Check whether hn_session_cookie is present and still logged in on Hacker News.

upvote

Upvote a story or comment by item id.

post_comment

Post a comment on a story or reply thread.

submit_story

Submit a new story link or Ask HN text post.
