What it can do

60 tools, in the server's own words.

send_message

Send one SMS from one of your numbers.

get_messageread-only

Retrieve a message by its msg_...

delete_message

Delete a finished message (delivered, failed or received) and every copy of its text, for a privacy request.

redact_message

Empty a finished message's text and media everywhere it is kept, keeping its status, price and timeline (redacted_at is set).

list_messagesread-only

List your messages, newest first.

send_verification

Send a one-time verification code (OTP) to a phone number by text, or to an email address with channel "email".

check_verification

Check a verification code submitted by a user, by the phone or email it went to.

fallback_verification

The text did not arrive: email a new code for the same pending verification (one id, one charge).

list_numbersread-only

List the phone numbers on your account.

request_short_code

Ask our team for a short code (or a toll-free number they set up).

list_number_requestsread-only

Open short-code and toll-free requests.

search_available_numbersread-only

Search purchasable numbers by area code (default 415).

purchase_number

Buy a phone number for your account (free sandbox numbers with test keys).

release_number

Release a number you own.

lookup_phoneread-only

Look up line type (mobile, landline, VoIP) and carrier for any US/Canada number.

simulate_inbound

Sandbox only: simulate an inbound SMS to one of your test numbers (emits message.received; STOP, START and HELP act as for real).

list_eventsread-only

List recent events (message.sent/delivered/failed/received, number.purchased/released, ...), newest first.

get_verificationread-only

Retrieve a verification by id: its status and attempts (never the code).

get_numberread-only

Retrieve one of your numbers: status, sender registration, tags, pools, customer.

bulk_purchase_numbers

Buy several numbers in one area code at once (e.g.

link_number_to_registration

Attach a number to an approved 10DLC registration so it can send that registration's traffic.

assign_number_to_customer

Platforms: attribute a number (and its messages and usage) to one of your customers, or clear it with customer_id null.

bulk_update_numbers

Act on many numbers at once: tag, untag, assign to a customer, or release.

export_numbersread-only

Your numbers as CSV text.
