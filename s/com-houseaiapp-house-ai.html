14 tools, in the server's own words.

search_design_stylesread-only

Browse House AI's interior design, exterior and landscaping styles (Japandi, Scandinavian, mid-century modern, coastal, farmhouse, industrial and more).

get_design_styleread-only

Details for one House AI design style: its color palette, materials, furniture, textiles, lighting, decor and what to avoid.

get_photo_upload_link

Make a private link where the user can upload a photo of their room, house or yard from their phone or computer.

redesign_room

redesign_exterior

AI exterior design and landscaping: restyle the outside of a house or building for curb appeal (siding, paint, trim, roof color, front door) or redesign a yard…

paint_walls

change_flooring

Replace the floor in a room photo with new flooring, to preview a renovation (for example 'light oak hardwood', 'large gray porcelain tile', 'herringbone').

empty_room

Remove the furniture and clutter from a room photo to see the empty space, for decluttering, moving or planning a new layout.

furnish_empty_room

Virtual staging: furnish and decorate an empty or bare room from a photo in a chosen style, for a new home, a rental or a listing.

find_furniture_and_decorread-only

Search House AI's shop for real furniture and home decor you can buy, like sofas, rugs, lamps, coffee tables, beds or wall art, optionally under a budget.

shop_this_lookread-only

Find real products that match an item in a room photo or a House AI design, for example the sofa, rug or pendant light in a redesign.

list_my_designsread-only

The user's recent House AI designs (room redesigns, exteriors, paint and flooring previews), newest first, with image links.

get_my_creditsread-only

How many House AI designs the user can still make today or this plan period.
