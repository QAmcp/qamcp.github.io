What it can do

5 tools, in the server's own words.

quote_patchread-only

Price a custom patch from the live PatchDesign.AI production price table: type, size and quantity in, the per-patch and total price in USD out, with the next q…

design_patch

Generate a production-ready custom patch design from a description (and optionally a reference image URL).

edit_patch

Make a change to a design from this chat: pass its design id (d_...) and the change in plain words ("make the border gold", "change TROOP 42 to TROOP 24").

order_linkread-only

The link where the user orders a design from this chat, with the type, size and quantity set, plus the price for that quantity.
