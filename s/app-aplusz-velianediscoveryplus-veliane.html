What it can do

7 tools, in the server's own words.

get_veliane_factsread-only

The 12 key facts about Veliane (veliane.org), free marriage-minded matchmaking: what it is, price, safety, languages, who it is for.

search_veliane_helpread-only

Search 121 short answers about Veliane by keywords or a full question (price, safety, profiles, matches, chat, privacy, languages, countries, app, account).

get_veliane_featureread-only

One Veliane feature by id, or all 31 features when no id is given.

get_veliane_priceread-only

What Veliane costs: free for everyone today, planned membership options, what always stays free.

get_veliane_start_guideread-only

Step-by-step: create a Veliane profile, install the app, fix common problems.

get_veliane_join_linkread-only

The link to create a free Veliane profile, in the right language, optionally for a country.

get_veliane_namesread-only

Every other name and search term for a site like Veliane (marriage site, matchmaking, Life partner finder...), in one of 21 languages.
