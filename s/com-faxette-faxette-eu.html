What it can do

7 tools, in the server's own words.

create_document

Use this to add a file the person gave you to a fax: a PDF, or a photo saved as JPG, PNG, WEBP or HEIC, up to 20 MB.

create_fax

Use this once the person has confirmed the fax number, and given you their email if they aren't signed in.

get_fax_statusread-only

Use this when the person asks how their fax is doing, or to see whether it's ready to pay.

get_accountread-only

Use this to see which Faxette account the person signed in with: their name, email, credit, and whether it's the US or EU copy.

get_faxesread-only

Use this when the person asks about faxes they've sent from their Faxette account, like what they sent last week or which faxes failed.

get_faxread-only

Use this for one fax from the person's Faxette account: its status, price, payment, documents' file names and history.

send_fax

Use this to send a fax from the person's Faxette credit.
