A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

16 tools, in the server's own words.

check_domain_email_setupread-only

Check whether a domain is set up to send and receive email properly: MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC in one go, each with pass/warn/fail, what was found, and how to fi…

check_dmarcread-only

Check a domain's DMARC record: whether one exists at _dmarc, its policy (none, quarantine, reject), whether reports are collected, and outdated tags.

check_spfread-only

Check a domain's SPF record: whether it exists, is valid, stays under the 10 DNS lookup limit, and ends with a sensible all mechanism.

check_dkimread-only

Look up a domain's DKIM public key, trying the selectors Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and common senders use, plus one you name.

check_mxread-only

List a domain's mail servers (MX records) and which provider handles its email.

check_bimiread-only

Check a domain's BIMI record (logo in the inbox) and whether its DMARC policy is strict enough for BIMI to show.

check_mta_stsread-only

Check a domain's MTA-STS policy, which forces encrypted delivery of incoming mail.

check_blacklistsread-only

Check whether a domain's sending IPs, or a single IPv4 address, are listed on the major email blacklists (Spamhaus, Barracuda, SpamCop and others), with remova…

analyze_email_headersread-only

Read the raw headers of a received email: SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, alignment, the delivery path and delays.

check_cold_emailread-only

Score a cold email (subject and body) out of 10 against the rules Pipefire checks its own emails with: length, reading level, the ask, subject line, and spam-f…

test_subject_lineread-only

Score a cold email subject line out of 10: length, wording that hurts opens, and spam trigger words, with fixes.

suggest_sending_domainsread-only

Suggest lookalike sending domains for cold email (so the main domain is never put at risk), with live .com/.net availability.

guess_email_addressesread-only

List the likely email address formats for a person at a company domain (first@, first.last@, flast@ ...).

generate_spf_recordread-only

Build a valid SPF TXT record for a domain from the email services it sends with, with warnings about the 10-lookup limit.

generate_dmarc_recordread-only

Build a valid DMARC TXT record (to publish at _dmarc.yourdomain) from a policy and report addresses.

generate_dkim_recordread-only

Format a DKIM public key as the TXT record to publish at selector._domainkey.yourdomain.
