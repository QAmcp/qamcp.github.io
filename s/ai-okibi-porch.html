What it can do

10 tools, in the server's own words.

create_inbox

Get a public URL that records every HTTP request sent to it, plus the inbox_key that all other tools need.

get_inboxread-only

Show an inbox: its URL, expiry, request count, latest request id, configured responses and approval links.

list_requestsread-only

List captured requests as summaries, oldest first.

get_requestread-only

Full details of one captured request: method, path, parsed query, headers, body (UTF-8 text or base64), plus "json" or "form" when the body parses.

wait_for_request

Block until a request arrives, then return it in full (same shape as get_request).

set_response

Choose what Porch sends back to callers, for the whole inbox or a sub-path: status, headers and body.

create_approval_link

Make a link to send a person when you need their decision ("Deploy to production?").

wait_for_approvalread-only

Block until the person answers an approval link, then return their choice.

clear_requests

Delete all captured requests from the inbox.

delete_inbox

Delete the inbox and everything it captured.
