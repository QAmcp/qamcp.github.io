What it can do

9 tools, in the server's own words.

lianex_browseread-only

List published browse categories with their slug, display name and updated_at freshness.

lianex_browse_by_slugread-only

Return ranked cached products for a published category.

lianex_product_by_safe_idread-only

Return one cached product and any stored cross-marketplace offers.

lianex_productsread-only

List published product permalink IDs and their updated_at freshness.

lianex_searchread-only

Search cached products using query words and, when recognized, their product category.

lianex_resolve_compareread-only

Resolve up to 3 comma-separated product permalink IDs into cached listings for comparison.

lianex_vsread-only

List published comparisons whose products remain cached, ordered by slug, with updated_at freshness.

lianex_vs_by_slugread-only

Return a published comparison with columns, rows, summary and product sides.

lianex_share_comparison_by_snapshot_idread-only

Resolve a historical shared-comparison token to its canonical comparison slug.
