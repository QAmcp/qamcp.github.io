A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

8 tools, in the server's own words.

get_daily_slateread-only

Games for a sport and Eastern date with start times, status, and plain-language rest and schedule context.

get_matchup_contextread-only

Schedule context for one game: each team's last result, rest, and next game.

request_capability

Tell us what you need that is missing: a category, sport, market, format and urgency, with an optional short plain-text note.

rate_response

Say whether a response was useful, with a reason code, tied to its request id.

calc_fair_valueread-only

No-margin fair probability from one or two of your own prices.

calc_parlayread-only

Combined price, and combined probability if you supply yours, for two to twenty legs.

calc_best_priceread-only

Rank up to 50 prices you supply for the same outcome by payout.

calc_ev_scanread-only

Expected value per unit for up to 50 price and probability pairs you supply.
