A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

6 tools, in the server's own words.

mappu_search_public_travelread-only

Search published Mappu pages by topic and language: audio guides to landmarks, walking tours, example itineraries and product help.

mappu_read_public_pageread-only

Read the text of a published Mappu page, such as an audio guide or a walk, in bounded windows.

mappu_find_walksread-only

Find self-guided Mappu walking tours for a city, neighbourhood or landmark, when the traveler wants a walk or asks what to see on foot.

mappu_get_audio_guideread-only

Look up a published Mappu audio guide to a landmark or place.

mappu_get_trip_planning_guideread-only

Optional reference for the format of a plan saved with mappu_save_trip_plan: how Mappu's own planner handles a day, timing and opening days, season, the places…

mappu_save_trip_plan

Save a trip plan made with the traveler into Mappu, where they keep trips as day-by-day itineraries on a map, edit them and take them along.
