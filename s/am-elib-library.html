What it can do

7 tools, in the server's own words.

search_booksread-only

Search the whole library: titles, authors and descriptions (Latin transliteration works too), then the books' full text.

list_booksread-only

Browse published books by subject, author or language, newest, most read or by title.

get_bookread-only

One book's details: title, author, subject, language, description, a passage of its text, its page address and related books.

list_subjectsread-only

Every subject that has books, with its id and how many books it holds, largest first.

search_authorsread-only

Find authors by name (Armenian, Russian or Latin spelling).

get_authorread-only

One author's biography, life dates, page address and books.

list_librariesread-only

The directory of libraries in Armenia that ELIB lists: name, city, address, phone, website, opening hours and map position.
