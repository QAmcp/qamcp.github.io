What it can do

18 tools, in the server's own words.

add_company_to_crm

Add a company to the customer's connected CRM from its own domain or public page link.

add_people_to_crm

Add up to 100 people to the customer's connected CRM in the background.

add_person_to_crm

Add a person to the customer's connected CRM.

confirm_possible_match

Record the customer's answer about a possible match from find_person_in_crm: that the CRM record is the same person, or a different one.

find_company_details

Find a company's details (industry, size, location, website, main phone line, technologies) from its own domain or its public page link.

find_contact_details

Find a person's work e-mail address, other contact details and current job from their public profile link, an e-mail address, or their name and company.

find_person_in_crmread-only

Check whether a person is already in the customer's connected CRM, by name or e-mail and optionally their profile link.

get_accountread-only

Show the Add to CRM account this connection belongs to: which CRM is connected, how many lookup credits are left, and anything that will currently stop a looku…

get_bulk_add_statusread-only

Check the progress of a bulk add started with add_people_to_crm: how many people are done, added, skipped as already in the CRM, or failed.

get_crm_recordread-only

Read one person or company record from the customer's CRM, as the CRM holds it.

get_profileread-only

Return the profile represented by this request's authenticated credentials.

list_listsread-only

Show the customer's saved lists of people and companies, with the id of each.

log_crm_activity

Log a note, call, meeting or task on a person or company record the customer's CRM holds.

save_to_list

Save up to 100 people that search_prospects or find_contact_details returned (each by its profile_url) to one of the customer's lists.

search_companies

Search for companies by name, business keywords, location, headcount or specific domains.

search_crmread-only

Search the customer's connected CRM for people or companies by name, e-mail address or company name.

search_prospects

Search for people to reach out to by job title, department, seniority, location and company details.

update_crm_record

Change a person's name, job title, e-mail addresses or phone numbers, or a company's name, website, industry or description, on a record in the customer's CRM.
