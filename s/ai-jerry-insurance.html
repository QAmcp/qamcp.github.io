What it can do

8 tools, in the server's own words.

jerry_get_accountread-only

Read the name, email and phone on the customer's Jerry account, and whether the phone is verified.

jerry_start_shopping

Start shopping for the insurance products the customer asked for.

jerry_update_application

Save application facts the customer gave, in their own words.

jerry_request_quotes

Request quotes.

jerry_request_final_price

Get the final price of the quote the customer chose.

jerry_waitread-only

Wait for quotes or a final price after requesting them.

jerry_get_statusread-only

Read where the customer's insurance shopping stands: the stage, what the current goal still needs (by application path), a summary of the application, what Jer…

jerry_get_quotesread-only

List every quote for a shopping intent, cheapest first by monthly price (in USD cents); each policy has a coverageSummary.
