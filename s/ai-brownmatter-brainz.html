What it can do

16 tools, in the server's own words.

search_conceptsread-only

Search the product knowledge vault by free-text query.

get_conceptread-only

Fetch one concept by exact id: full markdown body, metadata, and its direct graph neighbors (each with the relation that connects it).

get_relatedread-only

Walk the knowledge graph outward from a concept and return everything within `depth` hops, with distance and the edge path that reached it.

get_contextread-only

Assemble a markdown briefing of the concepts most relevant to a query and/or explicit ids, expanded through the graph and trimmed to a token budget.

upsert_concept

Write a concept note into the vault (creates or overwrites the markdown file, then reindexes it).

delete_concept

Remove a concept from the vault and all of its outgoing edges.

link_concepts

Add a typed, directed edge between two existing concepts by editing the `from` note's frontmatter (e.g.

list_conceptsread-only

Enumerate concept metadata (no bodies), optionally filtered by product, type, or a single tag.

vault_statsread-only

Summarize the vault: counts of concepts and edges, the products and tags in use, a breakdown by concept type, and whether semantic search is available.

onboard

Index an existing product's codebase into this vault.

sync

Re-index an onboarded vault after the code changes (drift).

dream

Dreaming: a scheduled consolidation sweep over this vault.

list_tasksread-only

List the vault's work pile: `task`-type concepts with their lifecycle (open/taken/done), oldest first, including who has taken each one (takenBy).

take_task

Claim a task and get its briefing in one call.

complete_task

Mark a task done.

create_task

Turn a finding into first-class work: creates a `task`-type concept with taskStatus=open and the given relations — part_of a domain, `realizes` stories/feature…
