get_article

get_law_texts

When calculated_current is present, use its text_ar for current monetary amounts and cite its authority.url.

get_ruling

Get one court ruling (judgment) by id: court, number, date, panel, and its verbatim text when the Centre holds it as typed text.

get_gazette_issue

Get one Official Gazette issue by id: its number, publication date, and the laws, decrees and decisions it published.

get_links

Get the graph edges into or out of one item: a law, an article, a ruling, an opinion, a research study or a Gazette issue.

list_legislation

List laws, decrees and decisions by date, newest first: for "everything published since January 2025" call with since "2025-01".

possibly_affected

Get the general repeals that may touch a law: earlier texts with a "all provisions contrary to this law are repealed" clause pointing at it.
