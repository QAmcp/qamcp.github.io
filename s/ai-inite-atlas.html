What it can do

26 tools, in the server's own words.

match_toolsread-only

Find AI tools that fit a described job, from the INITE Atlas - ten thousand tools re-read every week, ranked by measured use rather than votes.

atlas_lookupread-only

One AI tool from the INITE Atlas, by domain or name: what it does and who it is for, the starting price with the pricing page and the day it was read and how t…

atlas_searchread-only

Filter the INITE Atlas without a model: a phrase, a category, and slices that must all hold.

atlas_changesread-only

What changed among the tools in the INITE Atlas: prices up or down, free plans added or dropped, OpenAPI specs published, MCP servers listed.

atlas_couponsread-only

Live discount codes for AI tools in the INITE Atlas, optionally for one domain.

atlas_my_listingsread-only

The tools the signed-in account has claimed in the INITE Atlas: whether each claim is verified and how, whether the badge link is still on the site, premium un…

atlas_owner_claim

Start (or resume) a claim on a tool's Atlas card for the signed-in account.

atlas_owner_verify

Check the proof placed for a claim: method "badge" (with pageUrl, the page the badge link is on), "meta" (the homepage meta tag) or "dns" (the TXT record).

atlas_owner_cardread-only

For a tool the account has claimed: what the card prints and what it could not read - price, hiring, releases, company - with the page each was read from and w…

atlas_owner_set_read_pages

For a proved owner: name the pricing page, the careers page and the changelog or release notes, on your own domain.

atlas_owner_remeasure

Re-read the claimed site now - activity, hiring, releases, crawler policy, identity files - with the pages you named.

atlas_owner_statsread-only

Views of the card and clicks through to the site: thirty days on the free tier; ninety days and the shelf beside it on premium.

atlas_owner_propose_edit

Propose a correction a person at INITE reviews before it shows: field "name" (with name), "pricing" (with pricing: {model, freeTier, startsAt: {amount, currenc…

atlas_owner_couponsread-only

The coupons this claim has published, live or not, with their ids for atlas_owner_coupon_set.

atlas_owner_coupon_add

For a proved owner: publish a discount on the card and the Atlas coupon feed, shown as stated by the verified owner.

atlas_owner_coupon_set

action "confirm" says the coupon still works (it stops showing 45 days after the last confirmation); "withdraw" takes it down.

atlas_owner_premiumread-only

What premium adds (re-reads hourly, ninety-day stats with the shelf beside the card) and the link where the owner pays - checkout is a card form in a browser, …

atlas_owner_release

Give up the claim on a card.

atlas_compareread-only

Preview a price comparison of 2-5 Atlas tools for a usage scenario, computed without a model from plan editions checked against the vendors' own pricing pages.

atlas_dataset_create

Save a comparison as a private draft dataset in the signed-in account: tools, required capabilities, the default scenario.

atlas_dataset_update

Change a dataset the account owns: title, language, tools, required capabilities or default scenario.

atlas_dataset_publish

Make the named configVersion of a dataset public: cuts an immutable revision and returns the public API, page and compare URLs plus install notes.

atlas_dataset_getread-only

Read a dataset: a published one by anybody (its snapshot with freshness and the default result), a draft only by its owner (config, configVersion, preview).

atlas_dataset_changesread-only

Confirmed changes between the revisions of a published dataset, newest first: plans added, removed, or which fields moved.
