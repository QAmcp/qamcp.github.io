What it can do

4 tools, in the server's own words.

search_cardsread-only

Search Slabline's catalogue of 1.5M+ graded cards (PSA/BGS/SGC/CGC) by player, set, or year.

value_cardread-only

Get one card's current Slabline value: graded market value, raw/loose value, and the across-grades total.

searchread-only

Search Slabline for graded trading cards (sports & TCG) by player, set, or year.

fetchread-only

Fetch one Slabline card by id (the slug returned by search): its current graded market value, raw/loose value, and across-grades total.
