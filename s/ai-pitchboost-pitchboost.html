What it can do

7 tools, in the server's own words.

create_sample_deck

Generate a hosted, shareable sample pitch deck from a short prompt with no PitchBoost account.

create_deck

Generate a published PitchBoost deck from a prompt describing the prospect, offer, audience, and goal.

rebuild_deck

Improve, redesign, polish or rebrand an existing presentation.

get_rebuild_statusread-only

Check whether a rebuild started with rebuild_deck has finished.

publish_deck

Turn a deck's shareable link on.

list_decksread-only

List the most recent published decks for your team, each with its shareable link.

get_deckread-only

Get a single deck by its numeric id, including its shareable link and metadata.
