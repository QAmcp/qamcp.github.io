What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

7 tools, in the server's own words.

search_productsread-only

search the anne black catalogue by words (name, sku, colour, description), colour, category or max price.

get_productread-only

full details for one product by sku (a variant sku also works): description with dimensions, images, categories and every variant with colour, size, price and …

list_categoriesread-only

the shop categories (interior, tableware, jewellery, collections and their sub-categories) with ids for search_products.

get_shipping_ratesread-only

shipping price to a country by basket value, including the free-shipping threshold.

find_retailersread-only

physical shops that sell anne black, optionally filtered by country (english name, e.g.

about_anne_blackread-only

facts about the studio: what it makes, sizes and mounting of knobs and hooks, sites and currencies, shop address and opening hours, contact and wholesale.

create_cart_linkread-only

checks that the items can be bought (sold in the store, in stock) and returns a link for the customer.
