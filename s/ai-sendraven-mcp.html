What it can do

55 tools, in the server's own words.

send_email

Send a transactional email, immediately or scheduled.

list_emailsread-only

List messages newest first with their delivery status, including held, scheduled, skipped and failed ones and the mail campaigns and automations sent.

get_emailread-only

Fetch one message with its full event timeline (send, delivery, bounce, complaint, open, click).

cancel_scheduled_email

Cancel a scheduled email before it sends.

list_sending_domainsread-only

List sending domains with their verification status and the DNS records each one needs.

add_sending_domain

Register a sending domain and get back the DNS records to publish.

verify_sending_domain

Re-check a domain's DNS records now instead of waiting for the background monitor.

list_suppressionsread-only

List addresses we refuse to mail and why (hard_bounce, complaint, unsubscribe, manual, list_hygiene), newest first, each with its scope.

add_suppression

Stop sending to an address.

remove_suppression

Remove a suppression so the address can be mailed again.

create_broadcast

Create a campaign as a draft.

get_broadcastread-only

list_broadcastsread-only

List campaigns, newest first, with their status.

preview_broadcastread-only

How many contacts a campaign would reach, and whether the reputation gate would allow it.

send_broadcast

Send a draft or scheduled campaign now, or schedule it with scheduled_at.

pick_broadcast_winner

Decide an A/B test now instead of waiting for decide_at.

resume_broadcast

Continue a paused campaign now.

list_threadsread-only

List email conversations.

get_threadread-only

Read a conversation as a chronological transcript of outbound and inbound messages.

reply_to_message

Reply to a message, keeping it on the same conversation.

list_templatesread-only

List stored email templates and the variables each one needs.

render_templateread-only

Render a template with values, without sending, showing how the copy reads before it mails a real person.

send_template_email

Send an email built from a stored template.
