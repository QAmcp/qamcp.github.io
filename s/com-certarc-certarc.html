What it can do

12 tools, in the server's own words.

practice_questionread-only

Use this when someone wants CISM, CISSP, CISA, AAISM, or AAIA practice questions or sample exam questions, or asks for a quiz or to "test me".

check_practice_answerread-only

Use this when the learner has chosen an answer to a CertArc practice question.

get_exam_domainsread-only

Use this when someone asks what the CISM, CISSP, CISA, AAISM, or AAIA exam covers.

search_learning_resourcesread-only

Use this when someone asks for CertArc study guides on a topic.

open_study_panelread-only

Use this when the connected learner wants to see their CertArc study overview: progress, saved schedule and next study option for one certification.

get_study_patternsread-only

Use this when the connected learner asks why they miss questions, where their blind spots are, how their confidence matches their results, or which domains are…

get_readinessread-only

Use this when the connected learner asks about their readiness score, how it is built, how to raise it, or which sprint or exam to retake for the most points.

get_resultsread-only

Use this when the connected learner asks how they did on sprints, practice exams, the baseline or the final assessment.

get_result_reviewread-only

Use this when the connected learner asks what happened in one sprint or exam: which answers were Incorrect, their confidence, miss reasons, domains, knowledge …

get_study_summaryread-only

Use this when the connected learner asks about their CertArc progress.

get_study_planread-only

Use this when the connected learner asks what to study today or this week, what is overdue, or what is on their CertArc calendar.

get_next_study_actionread-only

Use this when the connected learner asks what to study next in CertArc.
