What it can do

4 tools, in the server's own words.

check_property_licensingread-only

Looks up a postcode in England in HouseFile’s records of council licensing schemes and returns each scheme in force there, with its dates, fee, source link and…

list_council_schemesread-only

Lists a council’s licensing schemes from HouseFile’s records: in force, starting soon, proposed, and ended in the last 12 months, each with its area, dates, fe…

upcoming_licensing_changesread-only

What’s coming: schemes proposed or starting soon, and recent changes to schemes in HouseFile’s records (started, ended, fee or area changed), newest first.

licensing_coverageread-only

Which councils HouseFile’s records cover, with the number of published schemes for each and when they were last verified.
