What it can do

5 tools, in the server's own words.

search_citiesread-only

Find the cities with an Atmy air-quality summary by name (any language) or country.

get_air_qualityread-only

get_air_quality_historyread-only

Daily PM2.5 of a city from the Atmy Data Hub (UTC days, sensors after the quality check), up to 400 days back.

get_country_rankingread-only
