A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

getCoinsread-only

List all available coins with id, name, symbol and rank.

getCoinByIdread-only

Get descriptive project information about a coin: description, links, team and tags.

getCoinEventsread-only

Get the timeline of events for a coin (launches, partnerships, hard forks, conference talks) with dates and descriptions.

getCoinExchangesread-only

List the exchanges that LIST a specific coin (venue names only).

getCoinMarketsread-only

Get the specific markets and trading pairs where a coin trades, with per-market price and 24h volume.

getCoinOHLCVLatestread-only

Get the latest full-day OHLC candle (open, high, low, close and volume) for a coin.

getCoinOHLCVTodayread-only

Get today's in-progress OHLC candle for a coin.

getCoinOHLCVHistoricalread-only

Get OHLC candles (open, high, low, close, volume) for a coin.

getTickersread-only

Get current prices, market caps and 24h changes for the top cryptocurrencies ranked by market cap.

getTickersByIdread-only

Get the current price, market cap, 24h change and volume of one cryptocurrency in USD or another quote currency.

getTickersHistoricalByIdread-only

Get a coin's price, market cap and volume at past times as point-in-time snapshots (not OHLC candles).

getExchangesread-only

List exchanges tracked by CoinPaprika as a list ranked by rank, each with id, name, trust score, active market count and 24h volume.

getExchangeByIDread-only

Get details for a specific exchange: 24h volume, market count, trust score, links and fiat support.

getExchangeMarketsread-only

List the trading pairs (markets) on one exchange as a list, each with the pair, base and quote coin ids, latest price, 24h volume and category.

getPeopleByIdread-only

Get the profile of one person in crypto by id: name, description or bio, teams_count, positions held, and linked projects plus social and code accounts.

getTagsread-only

List all CoinPaprika tags (categories such as 'defi', 'stablecoin', 'memecoin', 'ai') used to group coins, returned as a list with each tag's id, name, coin_co…

getTagByIdread-only

Get details for one CoinPaprika tag (a category such as 'defi' or 'stablecoin'): its id, name, description, coin_counter, ico_counter, and optionally the coins…

getPlatformsread-only

List the smart-contract platforms (blockchains such as ethereum, bnb, polygon) for which CoinPaprika indexes token contracts.

getContractsread-only

List the token contract addresses tracked on one platform as a list, mapping each contract to its CoinPaprika coin id.

getTickerByContractread-only

Get the current price and market data of a token by its contract address (for on-chain and DeFi tokens).

getHistoricalTickerByContractread-only

Get a token's price and market history by its contract address, as a time series.

statusread-only

Get the CoinPaprika MCP server's status and configuration (uptime, mode, enabled features).

getGlobalread-only

Get a global crypto market overview: total market cap, total 24h volume, Bitcoin dominance, and market-wide 24h change.

searchread-only

Broadly search CoinPaprika across coins, exchanges, ICOs, people and tags by name or symbol when the entity type is unknown or you are exploring.
