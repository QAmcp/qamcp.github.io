What it can do

26 tools, in the server's own words.

search_filingsread-only

Full-text search across all SEC filing content.

search_companiesread-only

Search for companies by name or ticker symbol.

get_companyread-only

Look up a company by CIK to get its name(s), ticker(s) and industry.

list_sic_codesread-only

List the SEC's SIC industry codes, so you know which code to use with list_companies_by_sic.

list_companies_by_sicread-only

List the companies in an industry, using the SEC's Standard Industrial Classification (SIC).

get_filingsread-only

Query SEC filings by form type, date range, CIK and/or 8-K item.

get_filing_detailread-only

Get full filing metadata including its content_object.

get_documentread-only

Fetch a filing document's content from storage.

list_filing_itemsread-only

List the Items of a 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K or 20-F: which sections the filing has and where.

get_filing_itemread-only

Read one Item of a 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K or 20-F, e.g.

list_filing_tablesread-only

List every R*.htm financial table in a filing (10-K, 10-Q, S-1, etc.).

get_holdingsread-only

Get parsed 13F-HR institutional holdings for a filer.

get_institutional_holdersread-only

Who holds a company, from 13F filings: how much of each share class institutions hold, and how that changed quarter by quarter.

get_insider_transactionsread-only

Get parsed Form 4 insider transactions for a company.

get_shares_outstandingread-only

Get a company's shares outstanding over time, as stated on the cover page of each 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F and 40-F (dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding).

get_fiscal_calendarread-only

Get a company's fiscal calendar: every quarter it reported, with its own dates.

search_private_offeringsread-only

Search Form D filings: private placements and private funds (exempt offerings under Regulation D, Section 4(a)(5) and the Investment Company Act 3(c) exclusion…

get_private_offeringread-only

Get one Form D filing in full, by accession number (from search_private_offerings).

get_financial_statementsread-only

Extract structured financial statements from a 10-K or 10-Q filing.

get_financial_historyread-only

A company's financial statements from its last several 10-Ks or 10-Qs, in one call.

search_documentread-only

Search within a filing's documents for specific terms.

verify_quoteread-only

Check that a figure or sentence really appears in a filing document, and get a link that highlights exactly where.

verify_figureread-only

Check that a number you are about to state appears in a filing, however it is written.

list_figuresread-only

List the numbers a filing document actually states, each with its label.
