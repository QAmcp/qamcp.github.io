What it can do

12 tools, in the server's own words.

search_busesread-only

Search the commercial buses currently listed for sale by participating dealers.

get_busread-only

One listed bus in full public detail, by its listing URL or slug, plus how to ask about it.

request_bus

Send a buyer's bus requirements to a CommercialBuses person, who searches participating dealer inventory and replies by email.

find_skoolie_busread-only

Find a bus to convert into a skoolie (a school bus or shuttle turned into a camper or tiny home).

check_cdl_requirementread-only

get_used_bus_pricesread-only

draft_board_proposalread-only

Write a bus purchase proposal for a church, school, camp or senior living board: the need, the bus, why not a 15-passenger van, the driver and license plan, a …

compare_own_vs_outsourceread-only

Yearly cost of owning a bus against charter, a contracted shuttle or rideshare, using only figures the caller supplies.

check_vehicle_rulesread-only

The US federal rules and published guidance that apply to a church, school, camp, senior living community, medical transport (NEMT) operator or hotel buying a …

list_bus_categoriesread-only

The commercial bus categories, each with what it actually is, typical passenger capacity, what operators use it for, and whether it needs a CDL endorsement.

get_bus_categoryread-only

One bus category in full, by slug, including the related categories worth considering instead.

get_buying_contextread-only

How sourcing works here, what capacity bands and fuel types mean in practice, and what the service does and does not do.
