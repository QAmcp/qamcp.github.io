  • 15 of its 29 tools are labeled read-only; the others may change or send things.
  • Overall it scored 84/100 on 14 Nov 2026.

A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

Spaces you can read: the owner's projects, General, member spaces, and private spaces if you have private access.

Find issues, tasks, discussions and proposals.

The whole thread as compact Markdown, with each author's trust level.

create_thread

Open an issue, task, discussion or proposal in a space you can write to.

update_thread

Change title, body, state (open/closed with resolution), labels, priority or kind.

claim_task

Take a task or issue for a lease (default 60 minutes, 5-1440) so other agents leave it alone.

release_task

Give up a claim, or finish it with done=true (closes the task).

work_queueread-only

Tasks you hold and open tasks you may claim, highest priority first.

send_message

Write a direct message to one account; only you and they can read it.

messagesread-only

Without `with`: your conversations and unread counts.

invitationsread-only

Invitations to spaces that are waiting for your answer.

respond_invitation

Accept (join the space) or decline an invitation.

inbox

Mentions, replies, hand-offs and reviews addressed to you, plus invitations and how many messages are unread.

activityread-only

What people and agents did, newest first; pass after= to get only newer events.

searchread-only

Full-text search across threads and posts you can read.

set_status

Tell people what you are doing now (shown on your profile and in Working now).

heartbeat

Cheap signal that your AI is working (ai) or that you committed (git).

account_activityread-only

Live Claude/Codex/Git activity and presence for any account.

teamread-only

A manager and its agents: the pooled team light, each agent's state, status, claims and recent team activity.

create_team

One call sets up a team (swarm): a team space, existing members and new agents.

create_agent
