A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

19 tools, in the server's own words.

list_companiesread-only

Who this connection acts as (user or API key) and every company it may act for, with read or read + write access in each (a.k.a.

list_shipmentsread-only

List or search the company's own tracked shipments, newest first.

list_shared_shipmentsread-only

Shipments that other companies (network connections) share with this company, read only, and open tracking requests (filter=requests).

get_shipmentread-only

Everything about one shipment: summary, route (POL, transshipments, POD with actual and estimated times), current position estimate, carrier ETA and predicted …

get_shipment_eventsread-only

The full DCSA-style event timeline of one shipment (gate in, load, departure, arrival, discharge, ...), oldest first.

list_arriving_shipmentsread-only

Active shipments not yet arrived whose carrier ETA falls within the next N days, soonest first.

list_delayed_shipmentsread-only

Active shipments whose carrier ETA has slipped versus the first ETA BerthTime saw, by at least min_delay_hours, most delayed first.

detect_referenceread-only

Check a number before tracking it: is it a container (ISO 6346 check digit), bill of lading or booking number, and which carrier issued it.

list_carriersread-only

The carriers BerthTime knows: SCAC, name, which reference types each supports and whether tracking is live today.

get_usageread-only

The company's plan and billable shipments for one month: included allowance, used, overage, cost so far and trial end.

list_alert_rulesread-only

The company's email alert rules (delay over N days, arrived, delivered, tracking failed), their scope (all shipments, a tag or a carrier) and how many people g…

list_support_requestsread-only

The company's support requests to the BerthTime team, most recent activity first.

track_shipment

Start tracking a container, bill of lading or booking number.

update_shipment_identifiers

Set, change or remove the booking and bill of lading numbers of a tracked shipment, or add container numbers to it.

refresh_shipment

Queue an immediate re-fetch of one shipment from the carrier.

get_share_link

The shipment's current public read-only tracking link (a URL anyone can open without logging in), if it has one.

create_share_link

Create a public read-only tracking link for a shipment, or return the active one (safe to repeat).

create_support_request

Open a support request with the BerthTime team on behalf of the user.

archive_shipment

Stop tracking a shipment.
