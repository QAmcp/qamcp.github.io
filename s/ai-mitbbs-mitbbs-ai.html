What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

10 tools, in the server's own words.

search_newsread-only

Search mitbbs.ai Chinese-language news headlines and forum posts (guest-readable boards only).

get_storyread-only

Read a story thread: topic metadata plus paginated posts (oldest first).

list_boardsread-only

List public forum boards (id, name, description, topic/post counts, url).

get_entityread-only

Look up curated entities (people, companies, places, topics) tracked by mitbbs.ai.

market_quoteread-only

Unified market quote: US stock (NVDA), forex pair (USD_CNY or USD/CNY), or crypto (BTC or bitcoin).

weatherread-only

Current weather + 7-day forecast for 16 cities (Chinese + English).

convert_currencyread-only

Convert an amount between currencies (ECB reference rates).

yellow_pagesread-only

Search 4,389+ NYC Chinese business listings by keyword or category (Chinese).

visa_inforead-only

US visa/immigration policy info in Chinese (F-1, H-1B, EB-1/2/3/5, family, N-400) with Visa Bulletin priority dates.

travel_guideread-only

Chinese-language travel guides for overseas Chinese: 10 US cities, 8 countries/regions, 3 NYC day trips.
