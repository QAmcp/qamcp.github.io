A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

8 tools, in the server's own words.

annsa_prioritiesread-only

Lists the ranked active priorities (open, ready, building) with trend, confidence and evidence counts.

annsa_specread-only

Full engineering spec for a priority.

annsa_askread-only

Answers a question about your feedback data (the default): search and synthesis across feedback, priorities, specs and outcomes, such as rankings, 'what can I …

annsa_work

Do the work on a priority, and resolve routine asks with undo.

annsa_file

Put new rows into the record: feedback or a finding, a priority from a title, a transcript, a direction doc's lines.

annsa_decide

Answer a Needs you question, sign a decision, approve or dismiss a proposed spec update, propose or retire a principle.

annsa_board

Arrange the board: stage, severity, merge, regroup, park, correct, bets, assign, retract, pick, cut, direction.

annsa_share

Tell the customers who asked that their priority shipped, by email or the widget.
