Search one Amazon store (22 countries; default amazon.pl) via SerpAPI, with every price also in PLN at the official NBP mid rate.

amazon_compare

Run one query on several Amazon stores in parallel (default pl, de, fr, it, es, nl; at most 10) and merge the results in PLN — the cheapest first by default.
