What it can do

25 tools, in the server's own words.

search_recipesread-only

Find recipes on Befana by keywords, ingredients, time or diet.

get_reciperead-only

One recipe in full, scaled to any servings, US or metric, with every amount written into the step that uses it, so steps can be read aloud as they are.

get_cook_stepsread-only

A recipe as hands-free steps for cooking along: a gather list, then one step at a time with its amounts, the ingredients it uses, its tip and any timer.

build_shopping_list

Merge recipes (each at its own servings) and anything else into one shopping list by aisle, in what a store sells (10 cloves garlic → 1 head).

update_shopping_list

Change a saved list after the cook answers what build_shopping_list or plan_meals set aside as likely_have, or says how the trip went.

plan_meals

Plan dinners and return the finished week in one call: picks by day (varied, perishables first, one new to the cook), merged shopping list (list), prep_order, …

update_meal_plan

Change the cook's saved meal plan in place: swap a day for another pick (constraints: faster, max_minutes, tags, query, or a slug the cook chose), drop a day, …

order_groceries

Send a shopping list to a grocer near the cook: into their cart where the store allows it (King Soopers and other Kroger stores, once connected), else one list…

draft_recipe

Turn a recipe the cook pasted, typed or linked into a draft to check: ingredients matched, steps in plain words with the amounts as tags, servings, times and t…

create_recipe

Publish a recipe to Befana.

generate_placeholder_cover

Draw a labeled illustration of the dish for a recipe the cook owns that has no photo, so it looks finished until they add a real one.

fork_recipe

Save the cook's own version of someone else's recipe, linked to the original, which never changes.

update_recipe

Change the cook's own recipe or version.

delete_recipe

Delete a recipe the cook owns, for good, when nobody else has touched it (no versions, reviews or saves by other cooks).

get_my_recipe_feedbackread-only

Read only the signed-in cook's saved private feedback and the recipe-as-written context (US units, original servings).

rate_recipe

Post the cook's star rating (1 to 5) and comment, and mark that they made it.

save_recipe

Keep a recipe in the cook's Saved list (My Kitchen on the website), or take it off with saved: false.

get_my_kitchenread-only

What the cook keeps on Befana: saved recipes, recipes they published, their own versions, shopping lists and meal plans, their ratings, a pantry count.

hand_back_recipe

Hand back a recipe the Befana team added to the cook's kitchen (get_my_kitchen newly_assigned) when the cook says "that one isn't mine".

get_my_statsread-only

How the cook's own recipes are doing: views, shares, prints, cooks, saves, versions and ratings, in total and by day, per recipe and by region.

pantry

Read or change the cook's pantry: food on hand and staples, which Befana leaves off shopping lists and uses for what-can-I-make.

suggest_from_pantryread-only

Recipes ranked by how much the cook already has, each with what's missing ("You have 7 of 9.

preferences

cook_profile

Read a cook's public Befana page, or change the signed-in cook's own.
