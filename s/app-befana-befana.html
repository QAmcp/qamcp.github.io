fork_recipe

Save the cook's own version of a recipe they don't own ("make it dairy-free", "halve the sugar", "scale it for 8 and keep that").

update_recipe

Edit a recipe the cook owns (one they published, or their own version from fork_recipe) in place: "add more garlic to my version".

rate_recipe

Record the cook's star rating (1 to 5) and optional comment.

save_recipe
