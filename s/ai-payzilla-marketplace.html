What it can do

4 tools, in the server's own words.

marketplace_search

Search the Marketplace listings by words in the name or description.

marketplace_get_listing

Show one Marketplace listing by id: price, seller label, whether Payzilla itself marked it, and where it pays.

listing_pay

Pay for one Marketplace listing with x402 (exact, USDC on Solana mainnet).

payment_status

Show the state of a payment you made with listing_pay, by the reference that listing_pay gave you.
