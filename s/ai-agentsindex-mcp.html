submit_listing

Submit a tool to AgentsIndex: the url alone; we write its name and description from its own pages.

send_lead

Send a buyer-intent enquiry (lead) to a tool or a whole category on AgentsIndex.

What a listing owner can buy on AgentsIndex, with prices in US cents (VAT included), each offer's term and what it needs.

A signed price for one offer (from list_offers) on one listing, valid for a short time, and whether the listing can have it now.
