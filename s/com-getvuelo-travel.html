What it can do

15 tools, in the server's own words.

search_tripsread-only

Find hotels, flights and flight-and-hotel packages on Vuelo for a destination, dates and number of travellers, within a budget.

get_hotel_availabilityread-only

Live rooms and prices at ONE hotel the traveller already has in mind, for their dates and number of guests, without searching the whole town.

get_offerread-only

Details, terms, photos and the payment options for one Vuelo offer, repriced live.

book_trip

Bookings are made under Vuelo's Booking Terms and Conditions (https://getvuelo.com/uk/legal/terms-of-booking-service/); the Vuelo card and pay page show them.

get_booking_progressread-only

Progress of the newest booking for an offer, or the newest booking of all, for the Vuelo widget.

change_payment_option

When the traveller wants to pay a different way (for example reserve now instead of paying in full) before they have confirmed.

update_travellers

Correct who is travelling on a booking the traveller has not paid for yet: a passenger's name or date of birth (names exactly as on the passport, dates YYYY-MM…

resend_link

When the traveller says the secure link has not arrived, or asks for another: Vuelo texts it again, to the same number.

send_to_payer

cancel_booking

Cancel a Vuelo booking, before or after the traveller confirms.

contact_support

Send the traveller's request to Vuelo's support team, who will be in touch with them.

find_my_booking

get_support_ticketread-only

Where a request to Vuelo's support team stands: with the team, replied (the reply is in the traveller's email), or resolved.

get_booking_statusread-only

The current state of a Vuelo booking started with book_trip.
