A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

21 tools, in the server's own words.

search_companyread-only

Search authoritative company registries by company name to resolve candidate legal entities and registration identifiers.

search_leiread-only

Find candidate LEIs by legal-entity name in GLEIF's global LEI index.

get_sec_filingsread-only

Find recent SEC EDGAR filing records for financial research, issuer due diligence and regulatory-event checks.

get_fx_rateread-only

Get a reference exchange rate, not a converted amount: supply base_currency and quote_currency as three-letter ISO codes supported by ECB via Frankfurter.

convert_currencyread-only

Convert a specified amount using ECB reference rates via Frankfurter.

get_company_profileread-only

Retrieve the Companies House profile for a known UK company number, including legal name, registration status, company type, incorporation date, registered off…

get_company_officersread-only

Retrieve directors and other officers recorded for a UK company at Companies House, including roles and appointment or resignation details where available.

get_company_ownersread-only

Retrieve Persons with Significant Control (PSC) recorded for a UK company at Companies House, including nature of control and available identity information.

get_company_filingsread-only

Retrieve recent Companies House filing-history metadata for a known UK company.

get_company_insolvencyread-only

Retrieve Companies House insolvency information for a known UK company.

get_entity_profileread-only

Retrieve normalized authoritative profile information for a resolved legal entity using its stable CelerAPI entity ID.

get_entity_officersread-only

Retrieve normalized authoritative management or registered-role information for a resolved legal entity where supported by its registry.

get_entity_ownersread-only

Retrieve normalized ownership or control records for a resolved legal entity where an audited authoritative registry supports the capability.

get_entity_filingsread-only

Retrieve normalized authoritative filing-history metadata for a resolved legal entity where supported by its registry.

get_entity_insolvencyread-only

Retrieve normalized authoritative insolvency information for a resolved legal entity where supported by its registry.

get_historical_fx_rateread-only

Return the European Central Bank reference rate for a three-letter ISO currency pair on a specified historical date.

lookup_leiread-only

Retrieve normalized public legal-entity reference data from GLEIF for an exact 20-character LEI.

get_lei_relationshipsread-only

Retrieve normalized direct and ultimate accounting-consolidation parent identifiers reported to GLEIF for an exact LEI.

search_sec_companyread-only

Search the official SEC company ticker reference data by issuer name, ticker, or CIK and return bounded normalized candidates.

get_timezoneread-only

Resolve an IANA time-zone identifier and convert an optional ISO timestamp using the Worker runtime time-zone database.

screen_walletread-only

Checks whether an exact digital-currency address appears in the authoritative sanctions datasets currently supported by CelerAPI.
