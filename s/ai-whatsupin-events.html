What it can do

5 tools, in the server's own words.

search_eventsread-only

Search every upcoming event in one What's Up In city (Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, Orange County, Sacramento, San Diego, San Francisco, Seattle, Albuquerque, …

list_upcomingread-only

The next N days of events in one What's Up In city, optionally one category, topic or place.

get_eventread-only

Look up one upcoming event by the `id` search_events or list_upcoming returned, with its source link and first_seen / last_seen / last_fetched dates.

search_across_citiesread-only

Search upcoming travel-worthy events across every What's Up In city at once (Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, Orange County, Sacramento, San Diego, San Francisco,…

list_citiesread-only

The cities What's Up In covers, each with its site, its own MCP endpoint, and how many travel-worthy events it currently indexes.
