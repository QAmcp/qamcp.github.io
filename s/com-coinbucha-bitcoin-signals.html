Ranked cross-signal feed of CoinBucha's leading, non-price Bitcoin indicators.

Hiring velocity across tracked Bitcoin and crypto-infrastructure employers, counted from their live ATS boards.

get_sovereign_reservesread-only

Nation-state Bitcoin holdings, ranked largest first.

sovereign_diffread-only

What changed between two frozen, immutable sovereign-reserve snapshots.

get_treasury_holdingsread-only

Bitcoin held on the balance sheets of tracked public companies, in aggregate and by largest holder.

Bitcoin network hashrate level and trend, derived from difficulty (expected, 2016-block epoch).

get_daily_brewread-only

The day's machine-readable digest of the whole signal set — the CoinBucha Daily Brew.

get_signal_historyread-only

Dated strength history for one CoinBucha signal, newest first.
