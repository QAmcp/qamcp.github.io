A test can only see what the server shows from outside, not what it does with your data behind the scenes. Connect servers from makers you trust.

What this test does not check

  • Its source code and packages.
  • What a tool quietly does behind the scenes, such as writing a file or calling another service.
  • Instructions hidden inside live tool answers. We only look for those in tool descriptions.
  • Hidden instructions worded in a way our list doesn't know yet.
  • Tools that could change something. Those are read, never run.

What it can do

22 tools, in the server's own words.

about_nexwinread-only

Explains what Nexwin is and what it does.

get_bookingsread-only

Lists the connected Nexwin account's appointments/bookings in a date range (max 30 days, first 25 results).

list_team_membersread-only

Lists the connected Nexwin account's active team members with their teamMemberId, name, role and timezone.

get_call_chat_historyread-only

Lists recent calls or chats for the connected Nexwin account in a date range, without full transcripts.

get_call_chat_detailread-only

Fetches the full transcript and detail for one call or chat, by id.

get_account_usage_summaryread-only

Plan name, subscription status, voice minutes and chat messages used in the current period, and wallet balance for the connected Nexwin account.

create_booking

Creates a new appointment on the connected Nexwin account and emails the customer a confirmation.

reschedule_booking

Moves an existing booking (appointmentId from get_bookings) to a new time and emails the customer about the change.

cancel_booking

Cancels a booking (appointmentId from get_bookings) by PERMANENTLY DELETING it — it will not appear afterwards, even as canceled.

get_activity_summaryread-only

Summarises what happened on the connected Nexwin account over a period: the answer to "what's happened the last two days?" or "anything I need to follow up?".

find_customerread-only

Looks up customers on the connected Nexwin account by part of a name, phone number or email address.

get_ai_setupread-only

Shows how the connected account's AI receptionist is set up right now, as callers hear it: business name, about text and address, the How to Handle Calls handb…

update_ai_setup

Changes how the connected account's AI receptionist answers, and puts the change live for real callers straight away.

restore_ai_setup_version

Undoes changes made with update_ai_setup or update_service_area by putting those same settings back to what they were in an earlier published version (version …

update_service_area

Changes which places the connected account's AI receptionist serves, and puts the change live for real callers straight away.

invite_team_member

Emails someone an invitation to join the connected Nexwin account with their own login.

add_team_member

Adds a person to the connected Nexwin account's team without a login, so bookings can be made with them.

update_team_member

Changes a team member's settings (teamMemberId from list_team_members): their timezone, role, how much notice they need before a booking (leadTimeMinutes), the…

remove_team_member

PERMANENTLY removes a team member (teamMemberId from list_team_members) from the connected Nexwin account.

list_assetsread-only

Lists the bookable assets on the connected Nexwin account: things customers book rather than people, such as hotel rooms, campsites, vehicles or equipment.

add_assets

Adds bookable assets (rooms, campsites, vehicles, equipment) to the connected Nexwin account, up to 100 in one call, and tells the live receptionist about them.

import_customers

Adds customers to the connected Nexwin account, up to 200 in one call, from a list the user has pasted or uploaded.
