What it can do

11 tools, in the server's own words.

list_covered_countriesread-only

The countries we research, each with its ISO code and one sourced line, and the mapped countries with the institution that pays their old age pension.

country_pension_factsread-only

One country's facts, the official free routes first, then what is hard from abroad by scheme and process, what the money is worth and dated obligations.

check_work_historyread-only

For each country in a work history: whether a pension is likely to exist and why, the official free routes first, what is hard from abroad and any dated obliga…

resolve_employerread-only

Places an employer name in a country and lists the employers we hold that it may mean, each with a confidence from 0 to 1.

authority_answerread-only

What a national pension institution told us in writing, as published on our answers page, with the institution, the date of the reply and the link.

deadline_checkread-only

The date a departure deadline runs out, from our deadline register and the date the person left or died, with the official source and read date.

create_order_link

Returns our order page with the report and countries filled in.

gpfr_inforead-only

What GPFR, the Global Pension Finding Register, is, in the caller's language, with the register page, the consent text and its version and, with a country, our…

gpfr_register

Registers a person in GPFR after they said yes to the consent sentence from gpfr_info, shown word for word.

gpfr_status

The status of a GPFR registration.

gpfr_fund_countread-only

For fund-side agents: how many confirmed people are registered for a pension fund, shown from 10 upwards, otherwise fewer than 10.
