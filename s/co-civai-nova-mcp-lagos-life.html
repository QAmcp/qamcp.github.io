What it can do

30 tools, in the server's own words.

login

Sign in to Lagos Life.

register

Create a new Lagos Life account (sign-up).

change_username

Rename the signed-in Lagos Life account (POST /api/auth/username).

session_status

Check whether you are signed in and which character you are playing as.

get_my_profile

Load your sim profile: avatar, bio, and account details from the game.

list_map_homes

Browse the city map: every home plot, who lives there, who is online, and who is at home (Lekki, Yaba, Ikoyi, and other areas).

list_contacts

List players saved in your in-game Phone contacts.

list_dm_threads

List your recent private message conversations from the in-game Phone app.

get_chat_with_player

Open one private chat and read the back-and-forth messages with another player.

get_daily_rewards

See today’s daily login rewards and streak for your sim.

get_forbes_leaderboard

See the in-game Forbes list of the richest Lagos Life players.

get_wallet_pending

Check pending wallet top-ups or purchases for your account.

get_wallet_and_job

See your in-game wallet balance in naira, your current job, and how your sim is doing.

list_job_careers

See available careers in the Jobs app (Tech, Banking, Music).

search_players

Find another player by username, like using Find a player on the in-game Phone.

get_home_catalog

List neighbour homes you can visit (GET /api/homes).

get_furniture_catalog

Buy mode furniture and décor catalog with prices in ₦ (static list synced from the game client).

get_home_layout

Your current home: placed furniture grid positions, storage, decor score, and cash.

list_neighbour_homes

Neighbour homes you can visit (GET /api/homes).

preview_home_design

Before/after 3D preview: Before = player's save as-is; After = ONLY items you place via home_design_placements (type, x, y, rot per piece).

propose_home_design

Same as preview_home_design (legacy tool name).

get_decorator_job_layout

Fetch a decorator job layout (GET /api/jobs?layout=).

apply_home_design

Apply home changes (PUT /api/save): add/buy, full layout, move, stow, or sell.

get_crime_record

Check your clean record, EFCC file, or police history in the game.
