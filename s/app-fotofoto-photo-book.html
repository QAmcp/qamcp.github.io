What it can do

8 tools, in the server's own words.

get_catalogread-only

List book types, shipping countries and photo requirements.

compare_photo_book_pricesread-only

Use this when someone asks what a photo book (Dutch: fotoboek) costs, how foto foto's price compares with other services, or which services sell a layflat book…

start_photo_bookread-only

Use this when someone wants to make a printed photo book (Dutch: fotoboek) or photo album but has not shared any photos yet.

create_book

Use this when the person wants a photo book (Dutch: fotoboek) made from photos they attached, direct image links, or a public iCloud Shared Album link.

get_bookread-only

Use this to show a book that was already made, or to get its price for one copy including shipping when country is supplied.

create_checkout

Prepare an optional Stripe checkout link after email claim, using the bookToken.

create_payment_quote

Prepare a fixed payment quote for a printed book, including shipping, for MPP and, when listed, x402 (USDC on Base).

pay_book

Buy the exact quoted book using native MPP.
