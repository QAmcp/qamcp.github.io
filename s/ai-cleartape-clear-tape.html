What it can do

11 tools, in the server's own words.

calculate_position_sizeread-only

Position size from the stop: how many shares to buy (or short) so that a stop-out loses a set percentage of the account.

calculate_risk_rewardread-only

Risk/reward ratio of a planned trade, the win rate it needs to break even, and (optionally) an exit expressed as an R multiple.

calculate_expectancyread-only

Expectancy per trade, per 100 trades, payoff ratio, break-even win rate and profit factor from a win rate and average win and loss.

calculate_atr_stopread-only

A volatility (ATR) stop: entry minus (long) or plus (short) a multiple of the average true range.

calculate_drawdown_recoveryread-only

The gain needed to recover from a drawdown and, optionally, how many losing trades in a row at a fixed risk per trade cause it.

calculate_kelly_criterionread-only

Full and fractional Kelly risk per trade from a win rate and payoff ratio, with the edge per 1R and the break-even win rate.

calculate_trading_costread-only

The cost of one round trip (commissions, bid-ask spread, slippage) in USD, in R, as a share of the target move and of the position, and over a year of trades.
