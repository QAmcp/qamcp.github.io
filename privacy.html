Privacy and terms, in plain words

Sheet 2 of 7 · The free tool

The terminal tool and the GitHub check

  • The terminal tool (qamcp-cli) runs on your computer and talks only to the MCP server you choose, and to that server's login service if it has one.
  • It sends nothing to us: no usage data, no reports, no error logs, no analytics.
  • If you log in with --login, the login pass is saved on your own computer in a .qamcp folder, and nowhere else. Delete that folder to remove it.
  • Reports are written only to the files you ask for, on your computer.
  • The GitHub check runs inside your own GitHub account, on GitHub's computers, under GitHub's terms. We receive nothing from it.
Sheet 3 of 7 · The website test

What happens when you test on qamcp.dev

The website can't reach your server by itself, so our test service (hosted on Railway) connects to it for you and sends back the results.

  • The report is kept: the server link and the results, so the result page and its share link keep working. It isn't linked to you, and report pages are hidden from search engines.
  • An API key you type is never saved. It's held in memory for that one test and dropped when the test ends.
  • A login pass ("It needs a login") is used for one test and thrown away. It's never written to disk.
  • Your IP address is held in memory for up to an hour, only to limit how many tests one visitor can start. It isn't saved.
  • Links that point into private networks are refused, so the service can't be used to reach machines it shouldn't.
  • No AI sees anything. Tool names, descriptions and answers are checked by plain code, never sent to an AI service.

For a server that shouldn't pass through anyone else, use the terminal tool instead: then nothing leaves your computer except the requests to your own server.

Sheet 4 of 7 · Monitor and payments

What Monitor keeps

  • Your email address, to confirm it's you and to send alerts.
  • Your server's link and its test history: every score, every change we found, and the alerts we sent.
  • A private link to your monitor page. Anyone with that link can see that page, so keep it to yourself.

Alert emails are sent through Resend, an email service. When you stop a monitor, testing stops; email us and we'll delete its history too.

Payments

When paid plans open, payments go through Razorpay. We never see or store your card number. We receive what we need to run your plan: your name, email, the amount, and whether the payment went through. Razorpay handles card details under its own privacy policy.

Sheet 5 of 7 · This website

No cookies, no ads, no tracking

  • No cookies for visitors, no ads, no tracking scripts, and no outside analytics service. The fonts are served from this site.
  • We count visits ourselves, as daily totals: which page was opened and which site sent you there (only its name, like "reddit.com"). We don't keep IP addresses or anything else that points to you.
  • If you ask to be notified when something opens, we keep your email (and the server link, if you give one) only to email you about it. We never share or sell it.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the domain and the hi@qamcp.dev mailbox run through Cloudflare. Like any host, they may keep basic technical logs to run and protect their services. We don't receive or use them.
Sheet 6 of 7 · Public servers and your choices

When we test public servers

  • We test public servers from the official MCP Registry, in safe mode (only tools labeled read-only, and never ones that sound destructive), and list the results in the directory.
  • To tell an owner what we found, we use the public contact details on the server's own website or registry page.
  • If you own a server and want us to stop testing it, remove a report, or delete the contact details we used, tell us and we will.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete anything we keep about you, email hi@qamcp.dev. We never sell or share your data, and we never use it for ads.

Sheet 7 of 7 · Terms of use

Using QAmcp

The software

QAmcp is free and open source under the MIT license. You may use, copy, change and share it. As the license says, it comes as is, without warranty of any kind.

Test only what you're allowed to

Use QAmcp on servers you own, servers you have permission to test, or public servers in their normal, intended way. You are responsible for how you use it, including any options that run more tools, such as --owner or --run-unlabeled. Use those only on your own server or a test copy.

Results

QAmcp reports facts it measured, such as pass or fail, timings and exact matches. It can still be wrong about a server, especially one that behaves in an unusual way. A report is a starting point to check, not a guarantee that a server is safe or unsafe. If a result looks wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

Names and other servers

Servers shown as examples on this site were tested independently. QAmcp is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, their owners.
