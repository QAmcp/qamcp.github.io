Sheet 2 of 2 · The rules

How it works

7-day money back on your first payment

For the first payment of a plan, monthly or yearly, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days. You don't have to give a reason. Email hi@qamcp.dev from the address you signed up with, and your monitoring stops when the refund is made.

Renewals

Renewals after the first payment aren't refunded, but you can cancel any time before the next one. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

How to cancel

Cancel from your dashboard, or email hi@qamcp.dev and we'll do it for you within 2 working days.

One-off purchases

For a one-off purchase like a security report: if we can't deliver it, or you ask before we've started, you get a full refund.

When we're at fault

If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or our service was down for a long time because of us, tell us and we'll refund what's fair, even outside these rules.

When the money arrives

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through Dodo Payments, who handle our payments. We start a refund within 2 working days of agreeing to it; your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 working days.
