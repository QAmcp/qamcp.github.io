Sheet 2 of 5 · The free tool

Using QAmcp

The software

The QAmcp terminal tool is free and open source under the MIT license. You may use, copy, change and share it. As the license says, it comes as is, without warranty of any kind.

Test only what you're allowed to

Use QAmcp on servers you own, servers you have permission to test, or public servers in their normal, intended way. You are responsible for how you use it, including options that run more tools, such as --owner or --run-unlabeled. Use those only on your own server or a test copy.

Results

QAmcp reports facts it measured, such as pass or fail, timings and exact matches. It can still be wrong about a server, especially one that behaves in an unusual way. A report is a starting point to check, not a guarantee that a server is safe or unsafe. If a result looks wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

Scores are never for sale

Every server in the directory gets the same test. Paying for a plan never changes a score, a ranking or a finding. The directory rules

Sheet 3 of 5 · Paid plans

Monitor and other paid plans

What you pay for

A paid plan gives you the features listed for it on the pricing page, such as how many servers we watch and how often we test them. Features marked "soon" aren't part of the plan until they launch.

Billing

  • Prices are in US dollars. Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, who sell paid plans on our behalf as the merchant of record and take care of sales tax and VAT. Your card statement may show Dodo Payments.
  • Plans renew automatically, monthly or yearly, until you cancel. Each renewal is charged to the card you signed up with.
  • A founding price stays the same for as long as your plan stays active. If you cancel and come back later, the price at that time applies.
  • If we change the price of a plan, we'll email you at least 30 days before it applies to you.

Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel any time. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. The first payment of a plan comes with a 7-day money-back promise. The details are on Refunds and cancellation.

Delivery

QAmcp is an online service: nothing is shipped. Your plan starts as soon as the payment goes through, and you'll get an email to confirm it.

Sheet 4 of 5 · Limits

Fair use and what we can promise

Fair use

Please don't use QAmcp to attack, overload or get into servers you don't have permission for, or try to get around its limits. If an account is used that way, we may stop it, and we'll refund the unused part of a paid period unless the use broke the law.

Availability

We work hard to keep tests and alerts running, but we can't promise they never stop. If something on our side breaks for a long time, tell us and we'll make it right, for example with free time on your plan.

Our responsibility

QAmcp tells you what it found; what you do with that is up to you. As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for indirect losses, such as lost profit or data, and our total responsibility for a paid plan is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the problem.
