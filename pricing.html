Sheet 4 of 4 · Questions

Before you pay

Does paying change my score in the directory?

No. Scores come from the same test for everyone and are never changed for money. Paying gets you monitoring and alerts, not a better number. The directory rules

What does Monitor do to my server?

The same as the free test in safe mode: it only runs tools your server labels read-only, and only when their name, description and inputs agree. It never runs tools that sound like they delete or change something.

What happens when the 50 founding spots are gone?

New customers pay $29 a month. If you started at $19, you keep $19 for as long as you stay subscribed.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your dashboard. Monitoring stops at the end of the month you paid for.

Is the free tool going away?

No. The test, the terminal tool, the GitHub check and the directory stay free and open source (MIT). Monitor is what pays for them.
