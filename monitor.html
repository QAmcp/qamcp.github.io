Sheet 2 of 6 · What it watches

What Monitor watches

  • Is it up?A quick check that runs no tools: every hour, or every 5 minutes on Pro for a server you own. After 2 failed checks in a row, you get a "Down" email; when it answers again, a "Back up" email with how long it was down.
  • The full QA testOnce a day on the free plan. On Pro, every hour on a server you own (every 6 hours until you prove it's yours). The same test as the free one: connection, MCP version, tool descriptions and inputs, safety labels, hidden instructions, bad-input handling, leaked secrets, speed, login setup, resources and prompts.
  • Score dropsWhen the score falls by 5 points or more, with exactly which checks got worse.
  • Tool changesA tool added, removed or renamed, an input or required field that changed, a safety label that flipped: the exact before and after, even if you didn't change it yourself.
  • Answers, not just "up"A server can answer and still be broken. You hear when a tool's answer changes shape, or it starts sending back a sign-in page, nothing at all, or far too much.
  • Login, MCP version, certificateWhen it suddenly asks for a login or its login setup breaks, when it goes back to an older MCP version (an update to a newer one comes as good news), and 14 days before its security certificate ends.
  • SlowWhen it takes 3 seconds or more to start and 3 times longer than usual, 2 checks in a row.
  • SecurityA hidden instruction that appears in a tool description, or a secret that starts showing up in an error message, and the moment it's gone again.
  • Gentle until you prove it's yoursWe never test a server hard unless its owner says so. Publish a short code on your server or in your DNS, it takes a minute, and you get full speed. If many people watch one server, it still gets one test.
  • Safe, alwaysOnly tools labeled read-only are ever run, and never anything that sounds like it deletes. Monitor never changes your data.

Sheet 6 of 6 · Questions

Questions

Does paying change my score in the directory?

No, never. Every server gets the same test, and scores are never changed for money. The directory rules

Can Monitor break or change my server?

No. It only runs tools your server labels read-only, and only when their name, description and inputs agree. The 5-minute check runs no tools at all.

My server needs a login. Does Monitor work?

Yes. On Pro, prove the server is yours, then save a key on its page and every test uses it. It's stored encrypted, sent only to your server, and never shown again. Use a key that can only read.

What do you keep about me?

Your email, your server's link and its test history. No AI reads any of it. Privacy

How is this different from an uptime checker?

An uptime checker tells you your server answered. QAmcp tells you an AI can still use it: tools, inputs, safety labels and answers, all compared with what your server normally does.
