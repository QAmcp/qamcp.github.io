The problem

Connect once, get everything

  • All or nothingWhen someone connects an AI app to an MCP server, they usually get every tool on it: the ones that read, and the ones that change or delete.
  • The same for everyoneA new hire and the team lead get the same tools. There's no simple way to say "support can read orders, only finance can refund".
  • No recordWhen something is changed or deleted, nobody can say who ran which tool, or when.
  • Secrets pass straight throughA tool's answer can carry a key, a password or a customer's details to the AI, and nothing checks it on the way.
How it works

One address, one rulebook

  1. Point your AI apps at GuardOne address instead of one per server. Guard passes messages on to the real servers.
  2. People sign in with their work accountGoogle or Microsoft, so Guard knows who is asking.
  3. Tick the tools each person or team may useA simple page with switches. One switch makes a whole server read-only.
  4. Everything else is blocked and hiddenThe AI never sees a tool it isn't allowed. If it calls one anyway, Guard refuses with a plain message.
  5. Secrets are hiddenKeys, passwords and personal details are replaced with [hidden] before the answer reaches the AI.
  6. Every call is loggedWho used which tool, when, and what was blocked.

Questions

Questions

Is Guard ready?

Not yet. It's being built. Join the waitlist and we email you once when it's ready to try.

Which servers does it cover?

The plan is every MCP server your team uses: ones you built, and outside ones such as GitHub, Jira, Slack and Notion.

Claude Code and Cursor already let me switch tools off. Why Guard?

Those switches are set by each person, on their own computer, one app at a time. Guard is one rulebook for the whole team and every AI app, with a log.

What can't Guard see?

Guard covers everything that goes through your MCP tools. A file an AI app reads straight from someone's computer doesn't pass through Guard.

How does it fit with QAmcp?

QAmcp tests a server and shows which tools are risky. Guard uses the same checks to suggest what to block.
