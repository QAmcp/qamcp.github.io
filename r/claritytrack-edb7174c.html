Test everything behind the login

One command runs every check above on the real tools. Nothing to install: npx downloads QAmcp and runs it (needs Node.js 20 or newer).

npx qamcp-cli https://www.claritytrack.ai/mcp --login --html report.html

Your browser opens the server's own login page. Your login stays on your computer and goes only to the server. The full report is saved as report.html in the folder you ran it from. Free and open source.

Privacy: nothing was sent anywhere except to the server being tested (and its login server, if it needed a login). QAmcp has no tracking.

Every result is a hard fact: pass or fail, milliseconds, exact matches. No AI judged anything. A check that could not run doesn't count either way; serious problems cap the score. How the score works.
